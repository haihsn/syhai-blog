I personally think it's not really possible to play pure GTO, like a machine, in
a live tournament. The reason is, live poker gives you so much information that
GTO just doesn't care about. You can see how your opponent talks, how fast they
bet, how they react in certain spots, small patterns in their behavior. All this
is data you can use to make a read.

Whether that read ends up correct or not doesn't really matter that much. What
matters is that you are acting based on your own read, not just playing a
default balanced strategy and ignoring everything you observe. That's actually
the whole point of exploiting someone, you deviate from the "optimal" strategy
because you believe you have information that makes the deviation worth it. If
you're wrong, you just adjust later. But if you never act on any read at all,
and just play solver-style the whole time, then you're not using one of the
biggest advantages live poker gives you, which is being able to actually see and
read a human being in front of you.
