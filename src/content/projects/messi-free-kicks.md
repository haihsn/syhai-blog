description: "An interactive map of all 74 direct free kick goals of Messi's career, plotted on a pitch with every dot coloured as the kit he wore for it, whether Barcelona, Argentina, PSG or Inter Miami. Filter by team and era, hover a dot for the match and its trajectory, then click through to the goal on YouTube. The match data is real, though dot positions are mostly seeded estimates rather than actual shot locations. Live on Vercel and still being refined."
